Where to look, in the order that pays off. Verified 2026-08-04; treat every date as
needing re-confirmation, and every URL as needing a liveness check.

### Creators / self-media

Productivity and AI-tooling creators overlap most with automation and digital-transformation
audiences. Record follower counts and whether they already follow you — a mutual follow is
a far warmer opening than a cold pitch, and it is the kind of detail an existing CRM
already holds.

## 5. Eligibility patterns worth memorising

- **Students-only is the default** for anything with 校園, 大專, 學生 or a university
  co-host in the name. Assume restricted until the 身分限制 field says otherwise.
- **"Open to all" (身分限制：不限)** is the phrase to search for. Corporate-sponsored
  data and AI competitions are the likeliest to carry it.
- **Government hackathons** are usually open to anyone but frequently require ROC
  nationality and cap team size.
- **International online events** are usually 18+ and country-agnostic; the practical
  barrier is the submission window, not eligibility.
- When a competition is closed to you, it is rarely worthless: mentor, judge, sponsor,
  set a challenge, or run a workshop. Record that route in the notes rather than
  deleting the row.
