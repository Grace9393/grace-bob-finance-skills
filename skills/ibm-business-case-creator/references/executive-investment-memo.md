# Executive Investment Memo

Use this framework when the user needs a short, executive-ready approval paper rather than a full business case.

## When to Use

- The user asks for a board paper, memo, approval note, or 1-2 pager
- The audience is an executive sponsor, CFO, board, or steering committee
- The decision window is short and the output must be concise

## Recommended Length

- 1-2 pages
- Short paragraphs and compact bullets

## Structure

1. Decision requested
   - What approval is being sought?
   - What commitment, funding, or authority is needed?
2. Why this matters now
   - Business problem or opportunity
   - Cost of delay
   - Strategic relevance
3. Options considered
   - Do nothing
   - Lower-cost or lower-scope option
   - Recommended option
4. Financial summary
   - Cost
   - benefit
   - payback / ROI
   - key assumptions
5. Key risks and mitigations
   - Top 3-5 only
6. Delivery and governance
   - High-level timeline
   - Owners
   - success metrics
7. Recommendation
   - Clear approval statement

## Output Guidance

- Assume the reader will skim
- Put the recommendation and economics on page 1
- Only include detail that changes the decision
- Move supporting calculations or evidence to an appendix if needed

## Common Mistakes

- Writing a long report and calling it a memo
- Hiding the actual approval request
- Burying assumptions or risks in prose
