# ROI Business Case

Use this framework for efficiency, automation, productivity, or cost-reduction opportunities where the decision mostly depends on quantified economics.

## When to Use

- The user wants a lean business case
- The decision is mainly financial rather than political
- The change is bounded and does not need a full strategic case
- The approver is a CFO, finance lead, or operational owner focused on ROI

## Structure

1. Executive summary
   - Recommendation
   - annual benefit
   - payback period
   - why now
2. Current-state baseline
   - Process volume, effort, error rate, rework, delays
   - Current annual cost in money and capacity terms
3. Investment required
   - Direct implementation costs
   - Indirect costs
   - Ongoing run costs
4. Benefits model
   - Cost savings
   - Revenue uplift or protection
   - Productivity gains translated into value
   - Risk reduction with monetary effect where credible
5. Options and recommendation
   - Do nothing
   - Manual optimization / lighter intervention
   - Preferred option
6. Risks and sensitivities
   - Assumptions
   - Best/base/worst case
   - Key downside protections
7. Delivery snapshot
   - Pilot or phased rollout
   - Quick wins
   - Success measures

## Output Guidance

- Lead with numbers, not prose
- Make every major assumption explicit
- Use conservative modelling where data quality is weak
- Show the bridge from operational metric to financial value

## Common Mistakes

- Claiming productivity gain without translating it into value
- Ignoring run costs
- Using percentage improvements without baseline volumes
- Recommending one option without showing the baseline alternative
