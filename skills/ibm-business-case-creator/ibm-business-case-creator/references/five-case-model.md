# Five Case Model

Use this as the default framework for formal business cases, public-sector style approvals, and multi-stakeholder investment decisions.

## When to Use

- The ask is a substantial investment or strategic change
- The approval path involves several stakeholders
- The client expects a formal, defensible structure
- The case needs strategic, financial, commercial, and delivery reasoning

## Structure

1. Strategic case
   - What is the problem or opportunity?
   - Why does it matter now?
   - How does it align to organisational priorities?
   - What is the cost of inaction?
2. Economic / options case
   - What are the realistic options?
   - What happens if the organisation does nothing?
   - What is the minimum viable intervention?
   - Why is the preferred option superior?
3. Financial case
   - What are the up-front, indirect, and ongoing costs?
   - What quantified benefits are expected and when?
   - What payback, NPV, IRR, or cash flow profile supports approval?
   - What assumptions and sensitivities matter most?
4. Commercial case
   - Why is this delivery model credible?
   - What partner, sourcing, or procurement choices reduce risk?
   - What protections, obligations, and responsibilities should be clear?
5. Management case
   - What roadmap, governance, and controls will make delivery succeed?
   - What benefits realization plan will track outcomes?
   - What risks and mitigations must be governed actively?

## Output Guidance

- Use explicit section headings mirroring the five cases
- Include an executive summary at the front
- Compare at least 3 options where possible:
  - do nothing
  - lighter / lower-cost intervention
  - preferred option
- Keep the recommendation evidence-led rather than sales-led

## Common Mistakes

- Turning the options section into a token paragraph
- Skipping the do-nothing baseline
- Treating commercial confidence only as vendor marketing
- Failing to name delivery governance, owners, and measurement

## Minimal Section Outline

```markdown
# Executive Summary

## 1. Strategic Case
## 2. Economic and Options Case
## 3. Financial Case
## 4. Commercial Case
## 5. Management Case
## Appendices
```
