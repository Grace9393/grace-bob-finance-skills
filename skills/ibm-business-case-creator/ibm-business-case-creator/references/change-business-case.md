# Change Business Case

Use this framework when the hardest part is not just funding the idea but securing stakeholder alignment, adoption, and organisational follow-through.

## When to Use

- The change affects roles, behaviours, governance, or operating model
- Leaders are concerned about adoption risk or disruption
- Benefits depend heavily on people changing how they work
- The user needs a persuasive case for transformation, not just tooling

## Structure

1. Executive summary
   - Recommendation
   - case for change
   - headline value
   - adoption confidence
2. Case for change
   - Current-state pain and strategic pressure
   - Why the current operating model is no longer sufficient
   - Why now
3. Stakeholder impact
   - Who is affected
   - What each stakeholder group gains or fears
   - Likely objections and resistance points
4. Options and transformation path
   - Do nothing
   - Incremental change
   - Preferred transformation path
5. Value and investment
   - Costs
   - Quantified and non-quantified benefits
   - How adoption influences value realization
6. Change and adoption plan
   - Sponsorship
   - Communications
   - Training and enablement
   - Reinforcement and feedback loops
7. Governance, risk, and measures
   - Decision forums
   - Change risks
   - Adoption KPIs
   - Benefits tracking

## Output Guidance

- Treat resistance as normal and address it directly
- Connect value to adoption, not just technology deployment
- Give leaders confidence that the organisation can absorb the change

## Common Mistakes

- Writing a technology case when the real barrier is behaviour change
- Assuming training alone solves adoption
- Ignoring political history from failed prior initiatives
