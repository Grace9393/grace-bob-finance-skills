## Script

**[1 — 0:00] Grace, over the hand-built blueprint:**
"This is a finance-transformation blueprint — process narrative, personas, pain points, agents, benefits. Clients buy these. And this one took a senior consultant more than a day to hand-assemble: research, schema, structure, deck. We produce dozens per engagement, they all come out slightly different, and the knowledge gets rebuilt from zero every time. Bob exists to enable coordinated, multi-step change with governance. That's literally what we sell. So we pointed Bob at our own work."

**[2 — 0:30] The workbench:**
"One website, built with Bob. I set the client once — name, ticker, domain, process area — and everything builds itself: targeted searches for the official org chart, the latest financial statements, Gartner reprints. A starter master schema, downloadable as JSON-LD. And the Bob prompt, pre-filled. Timer starts now."

**[3 — 1:00] Bob + validator:**
"One prompt, one run — Bob researches the client, captures the org chart as Executive entities, the annual report as Report entities, and extends our practice base ontology. No back-and-forth: the contract says record your assumptions, don't ask me questions. Back in the workbench, I paste Bob's schema into the validator — it catches everything that makes a Context Studio import fail silently. Clean. Download."

**[4 — 2:15] ICA:**
"Into IBM Consulting Advantage. Context Studio imports the schema, ingests the research corpus, and the knowledge graph populates — I verify with three grounding questions. Then the Agentic App Studio workflow we built takes over: our Blueprint Generator agent drafts the cognitive decomposition, agent register, and operating model. Our Method Pressure-Tester agent checks it against IBM method — and it's flagged one deviation. The workflow pauses. That's the point: the approval gate is part of the chain, nobody has to remember to review." *(clicks)* "Fix accepted. Signed off."

**[5 — 3:15] The deck:**
"Bob renders the signed-off blueprint into our branded client deck. And see this marker — SENIOR REVIEW — where the blueprint didn't have what a slide needed, Bob flagged it instead of inventing content. Timer stops: [say the actual elapsed minutes shown on the workbench timer — never a rehearsed number]." 

**[6 — 3:45] Two decks side by side:**
**Joe:** "This run is my scenario — a Finance Resolution Agent orchestrating exceptions across OTC, PTP, RTR and FP&A."
**Kate:** "And this one is mine — a CFO co-pilot for insight-to-action. Same workflow, zero changes, different process area."
**Grace:** "Two runs, one unchanged pipeline, structurally identical outputs. That's repeatability — and every teammate's idea is in the demo."

**[7 — 4:30] Metric slide:**
"Before: a day-plus of hand-assembly per use case. After: [the actual timer total] of review-and-orchestrate — you watched the timer. Every client schema joins a compounding practice library, validator-gated so it scales. We run this on every process area of every engagement after July 22 — with IBM Bob and the three ICA studios exactly as they exist today. Thank you."
